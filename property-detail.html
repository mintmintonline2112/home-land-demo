Description

This stunning garden villa sits in Barton Hills — one of Austin's most sought-after neighborhoods, just 10 minutes from downtown and steps away from Zilker Park. The modern open-concept design floods every room with natural light and blends indoor living seamlessly with the landscaped backyard.
