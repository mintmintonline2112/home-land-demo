Realtor® · First Team Real Estate

Sean Truong

Serving Orange County & the Bay Area

Sean began his real estate career over 15 years ago with a simple belief: buying a home in America should feel exciting, not overwhelming. Since then, he has guided more than 350 families through successful purchases and sales — from first condos in San Jose to estate homes in Newport Beach and Villa Park.

As a bilingual Vietnamese-American agent, Sean is especially proud to serve the Vietnamese community, walking clients through every document and decision in the language they're most comfortable with. For international buyers, he partners with trusted immigration and financial professionals to make the move to California seamless.
