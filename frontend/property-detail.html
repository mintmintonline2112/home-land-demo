Description

Nestled in Irvine's prestigious Turtle Rock community, this beautifully updated estate offers panoramic hillside views, award-winning schools, and quick access to UC Irvine, fine dining, and the coast. The open-concept design floods every room with California sunshine.

The main level features a dramatic two-story foyer, a chef's kitchen with a large center island, and a great room that opens to a resort-style backyard with pool, spa, and covered California room. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a spa bath, walk-in closet, and a private view balcony. A main-floor bedroom and full bath complete the layout — perfect for guests or multi-generational living.
